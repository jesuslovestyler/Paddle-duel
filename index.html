How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the court (finger or mouse) to slide your glowing paddle along the bottom. It follows your finger exactly — no lag, no drift.
  • On a keyboard, use the ← and → arrow keys.
  • The ball bounces off the side walls and both paddles. Where it hits your paddle sets the angle — clip the edge to fire a sharp shot, meet it dead-center to send it straight back.
  • Every paddle hit makes the ball a little faster. It resets when a point is scored.
  • A point is scored when the ball slips past a paddle. First to 7 wins the duel.
  • Watch out: the CPU reads the ball faster as your score climbs, so the last points are the hardest to earn.
